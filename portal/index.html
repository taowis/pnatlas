GeneStatesAbout and downloads

Specificity is the share of a gene's expression that falls in one state across the 31 reference states (the values sum to 1 over the states). Log CPM is the gene's expression in the state's pseudobulk. The GTEx column is the Spearman correlation across 670 tibial nerve samples between the gene's expression and the estimated fraction of each nerve state.

Reference states

Cells and known donors per state in the joint ganglion and nerve reference; whether the state was placed in tissue in the spatial sections of each compartment; its mean fraction in GTEx tibial nerve (MuSiC is the primary estimate; BayesPrism is kept for the rare states that MuSiC sets to zero); and the neuropathy gene classes enriched among its specific genes.
